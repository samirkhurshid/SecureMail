Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 10, 2026

At SecureMail, transparency and security are fundamental to our mission. This Privacy Policy explains exactly what data we collect, how we process it, where it is stored, how long it is retained, and how you can exercise full control over your personal information.

1. Information We Collect

We collect only the information necessary to provide email threat detection, forensic analysis, and account management:

  • Account Information: Email address, optional display name, creation timestamp, and authentication provider details (Firebase Auth / Google OAuth).
  • Scanned Email Metadata & Content: Email headers (From, To, Subject, Received hops, SPF/DKIM/DMARC verdicts), email body text, extracted URLs, and attachment SHA-256/MD5 hashes. Note: Full attachment files uploaded for scanning are analyzed strictly in-memory to compute hashes and are never stored on disk.
  • Session & Security Metadata: Client IP address and User-Agent string recorded upon user sign-in. This metadata is encrypted at rest using symmetric Fernet encryption before storage.
  • User Preferences: Weekly email digest opt-in preference and user-configured Slack/Webhook notification URLs.

2. How We Use Your Information

We process your data strictly to deliver SecureMail's core security services:

  • Analyzing emails, headers, URLs, and attachment hashes for phishing, spoofing, and malware threats.
  • Generating user-scoped forensic scan logs accessible via your dashboard.
  • Delivering automated weekly threat digest emails (if opted-in).
  • Sending real-time Slack/Webhook notifications for high or critical risk threat detections.

3. Third-Party Service Providers

To analyze threats accurately, SecureMail integrates with select specialized third-party APIs. Data shared with these services is strictly limited to the technical fields required for security analysis:

Provider Purpose Data Shared
Firebase (Google) User authentication & document database Email address, user ID, account metadata
VirusTotal URL & attachment malware reputation checks URLs found in emails, attachment SHA256 hashes
AbuseIPDB Originating IP reputation analysis Originating IP address extracted from email headers
Anthropic / Google Gemini AI Threat Explainer analysis Aggregated threat indicators, headers & risk summary
Resend Transactional weekly threat digest emails Recipient email address, weekly threat summary HTML

4. Anonymous Free Trial Privacy Zero Storage

Visitors using SecureMail's Anonymous Free Trial (5 scans per day per IP address) enjoy complete privacy. Scanned email content, URLs, and analysis results from trial scans are processed in-memory and never saved to disk or persisted to forensic log files. Trial usage is tracked statelessly in a local counter database storing only the requesting IP address and daily count.

5. Data Retention & 7-Day Account Purge

  • Forensic Scan Logs: Saved to disk in your personal log folder while your account is active. Logs remain stored until you individually delete them or request account deletion. Forensic log files on disk are stored in standard JSON format.
  • Account Deletion (7-Day Grace Period): When you request account deletion from your Account page, your refresh tokens are revoked immediately, locking your session. Your account enters a 7-day soft-delete grace period during which you can contact support if initiated by mistake. After 7 days, an automated purge permanently deletes your Firebase Auth record, profile documents, and all on-disk forensic log files. Purge operations are recorded in an audit log (purge_audit.log).

6. Security Controls

We implement multiple layers of security to protect your data:

  • Metadata Encryption at Rest: Sign-in session metadata (IP address and User-Agent) is symmetrically encrypted using Fernet base64 keys before storage.
  • Server-Side API Key Protection: All third-party API keys remain securely on the server and are never exposed to the client or browser.
  • Transport Security: All client-server communications are transmitted via TLS/HTTPS. Security headers (Content-Security-Policy, X-Content-Type-Options, X-Frame-Options, Referrer-Policy) are enforced across all endpoints.

7. Your Data Rights

Under applicable privacy laws (including GDPR), you retain full rights over your data:

  • Right to Access & Export: You can download a complete JSON archive of your profile, all forensic logs, and decrypted login history at any time via the "Download My Data" button on your Account page.
  • Right to Rectification & Deletion: You can update preferences, clear webhook configurations, delete individual forensic logs, or schedule full account deletion directly from the dashboard.
